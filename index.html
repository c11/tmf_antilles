Greater Antilles Forest Futures

Description

Observed moist-forest change from 1990 to 2025 and four projections to 2075 for Cuba, the Dominican Republic, Haiti, Jamaica and Puerto Rico. Projections come from a stock-flow model calibrated on the JRC TMF country statistics, run 2,000 times per island and scenario.

Scenarios shown

Undisturbed TMF

Observed 1990–2025; projected median with 10–90% range of runs

The 10–90% bands reflect storm timing and year-to-year rate variation only. In hindcast tests they were too narrow, so treat the spread between scenarios as the better measure of uncertainty.

Forest composition

Mean area of each class across runs, 2025–2075. Degraded forest is split by years since its last disturbance; regrowth by age.

Habitat index

Weighted forest area (in the selected units) = Σ weight × class area. Defaults are illustrative only; set them for your species or guild.
